# Read aloud

Read aloud turns a reply Claude wrote on screen into a few spoken words for Alex. He hears them on his headset and may not be looking at the screen.

## What to read aloud

The last reply Claude gave Alex, as he would see it at the end of the turn.

## What to say

- Do not read the reply and do not summarize it.
- Tell him only what he must do or answer: the decision, question or action the reply asks of him, with just enough context to act on it.
- If the reply asks nothing of him, say in one sentence what changed for him.
- One or two sentences. What he must do comes first.

## How to say it

- Plain spoken English, as one person talking to another.
- No markdown, lists, paths, code, identifiers, or numbers he would need to see.
- Never point at the screen.
- Never repeat his own words back to him. When the reply quotes or answers what he said, say only what follows for him.
- Answer with the spoken text alone.
